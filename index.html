Rules

REVOLUTION server rules.

01

Teaming And Alliance Rules

  • 1.1Teaming is strictly prohibited. Teaming means actively choosing not to engage or kill an enemy player from a different Hive when you have a clear opportunity to do so.
  • 1.2No temporary alliances in combat. Forming temporary alliances during active fights, including teaming up mid-combat against a player you dislike, is not allowed.
  • 1.3No team swapping for advantages. Leaving and rejoining teams to gain an advantage is not permitted.
  • 1.4No exceeding team limits. Teaming beyond the allowed player limit is strictly forbidden.
  • 1.5No switching partners for advantages. Switching partners to gain an advantage is prohibited.

You are allowed to switch teams one time per wipe for any reason. If you need to switch again, you must notify the admins.

Rule violation penalties

1st offense: Warning.

2nd offense: 6-hour ban.

3rd offense: 6-hour ban + character progress reset.

02

Trading

  • 2.1Opposing parties may transfer goods between players outside their Hive only if it happens in safe areas through the trading system.
  • 2.2Personal vending machines are allowed, but server staff are not responsible for lost goods if the vending machine is raided.
03

Chat And Community Behavior

  • 3.1The game can become toxic sometimes, but everyone is expected to keep a respectful attitude.
  • 3.2No harassment in game chat or Discord. Proof may be required.
  • 3.3Spreading false information about REVOLUTION is not allowed.
  • 3.4Promoting or discussing other servers is not allowed.
Rule violation penalties

Depending on the gravity of the situation: server mute for a period of time, warning, temporary or permanent Discord server ban, or temporary or permanent game server ban.

04

Bugs

  • 4.1Using any type of bug is strictly forbidden. It will result in a permanent ban + character deletion.
05

Building • Bugs • Cheating

Once Human REVOLUTION is still being improved. Please report bugs so we can make the server better together.

  • BuildDo not glitch foundations into terrain, textures, or terminals. Using terrain or elevation exploits for unfair defense is strictly prohibited.
  • BasesNo glitched or buggy bases. Stacked foundations, clipping structures into map textures, or hiding the territory terminal inside foundations is strictly forbidden.
  • LimitsNo territory or limit exploits. Merging territories to bypass building or turret limits is considered bug abuse.
Rule violation penalties

Wipe progression deleted.

6-hour ban.

06

Building Areas

  • 5.1Building outside permitted areas is not allowed.
07

Daily Kit And Weekly Kit

  • 6.1The REVOLUTION custom server shopping system uses Starchrom to purchase specialized progression kits.
  • KitsAvailable kits can be purchased from the game shop.
  • CurrencyAdmin staff will frequently send Starchrom directly to players.
  • DeliveryCheck your in-game mailbox and claim your Starchrom.
08

Player Names

  • 7.1Unreadable or strange names are not allowed, including names using Chinese or Japanese characters.
  • 7.2Impersonating yourself as a cheater or admin will result in a permanent ban + character deletion.
09

Meteor Car

  • 8.1The METEOR CAR cannot be used for raids.
10

Staff Rules

  • 9.1Staff members must always remain fair and neutral.
  • 9.2No interference. Staff must not interfere in player fights.
  • 9.3Base locations. Staff must never reveal or hint at player base locations.
